17Evidence ledger

Every source, assumption and hypothesis, in one place.

Nothing in this plan asks to be taken on trust. Public sources are linked. What came from our conversation is marked as shared. Planning inputs say how they get validated, and hypotheses say how they get tested.

    How this was made

    Research was done on October 11 and 12, 2026, from public sources and one conversation. I built the site with Claude Code, an AI coding tool, as a research and build partner. Benchmarks come from named third parties and are used as starting points, not predictions. Concept ads and the dashboard use invented data and say so.

    What this page does not do

    It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects nothing. Fonts are self-hosted and every chart is drawn in the browser. It is marked to stay out of search engines.

    What is not here

    Internal data. Every conversion rate, cost and capacity figure is a planning input until the week-one audit and the trial backfill replace it with Control Bionics' own numbers.

Each of the three channels has its own job. LinkedIn reaches the named accounts, search catches people already looking, and email turns interest into booked calls.

The other 25% of channel spend goes to retargeting (10%) and small capped tests (15%), such as newsletter sponsorships, co-marketing with testing vendors and the CSM booth.

Retargeting
Meta and YouTube ads to site visitors, video viewers and our contact lists. They keep us in front of people between touches. They are not used to find new prospects.
APTA CSM in February
The Combined Sections Meeting runs February 4 to 6, 2027, in Philadelphia. We book meetings with target accounts before the show, run a 60-second muscle test at the booth, and follow up every conversation within 48 hours. Event costs sit in their own budget.
What we are holding back on
Meta and Instagram prospecting, paid YouTube reach, content syndication, and any patient or consumer advertising. Meta prospecting gets a small test in month three if retargeting works.

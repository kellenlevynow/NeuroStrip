02The idea

Run the demand engine the way NeuroStrip reads a muscle.

The story Control Bionics tells teams is about a measure that looked fine and a signal that did not. Demand generation has the same trap. Lead volume is the force plate: easy to hit, easy to trust, and able to hide a pipeline that is compensating. This plan instruments the signal.

{{i:scan}}

Measure the signal

Every lead carries its audience, message, channel and campaign into Salesforce and through to revenue. Cost per lead stays off the scorecard. As a diagnostic, it is never shown without the sales-accepted rate beside it.

{{i:hourglass}}

Feed the constraint

The bottleneck is not traffic. It is one inside-sales calendar, a finite pool of pilot kits and a thin proof library. Marketing sends the best 20 conversations a week, not the most.

{{i:trending-up}}

Earn the scale

Spend follows evidence. More than a third of the budget buys proof and measurement before it buys reach, and media grows only as tests clear rules written in advance.

Six mental models the plan leans on

  • Output is not signalJudge the system by what happens underneath the easy number.
  • Theory of constraintsA system moves at the speed of its tightest point. Find it, feed it, then widen it.
  • The 95:5 ruleAbout 5% of buyers are in-market at any time.{{ev:v:s37}} Capture them with search. Build memory with the rest so they call first.
  • Awareness before askMatch the message to what the buyer already knows. No demo ads to people who do not know the problem exists.
  • Barbell budgetMost of the money in named accounts and proven capture. A small, capped share in bets that could change the slope.
  • Land and expandOne clinic, 30 days, agreed criteria. Then the group.

06The system

One machine. Seven stations. Three loops that make it smarter.

A campaign ends. A system learns. This is the path a stranger takes to become a customer, what gets written down at each step, and how results travel back upstream to change what happens next week.

  1. 1{{i:megaphone}}

    Reach

    Named accounts see one idea, more than once, from more than one direction.

    Recorded as
    Campaign and member status
    Measured by
    Tier A accounts reached 3+ times
  2. 2{{i:eye}}

    Engage

    They watch the trace, read the guide, search the category.

    Recorded as
    Engagement history on the account
    Measured by
    Engaged accounts per week
  3. 3{{i:inbox}}

    Capture

    A hand goes up: a form, a reply, a badge scan, a booking.

    Recorded as
    Lead with source, UTMs and self-reported source
    Measured by
    Tier A and B share of leads
  4. 4{{i:filter}}

    Qualify

    Fit and intent decide the route: calendar, group demo or nurture.

    Recorded as
    Lifecycle stage with timestamp, grade, score
    Measured by
    MQL to sales-accepted rate
  5. 5{{i:phone}}

    Converse

    A discovery call inside the response window, scored against PILOT.

    Recorded as
    Event, outcome, checklist, opportunity
    Measured by
    Held calls a week, time to first touch
  6. 6{{i:flask-conical}}

    Prove

    Thirty days at one clinic, against criteria agreed before it starts.

    Recorded as
    Opportunity stage, pilot dates, criteria
    Measured by
    Pilot starts, pilot to paid
  7. 7{{i:network}}

    Expand

    Clinic two through twenty-five, and a story for the next account.

    Recorded as
    Account hierarchy, sites live
    Measured by
    Sites live per account, revenue
{{i:refresh-cw}}
Creative loop weekly

Results by territory, persona and format decide next week's creative. Stations 1 to 3.

{{i:refresh-cw}}
Lead-quality loop weekly, with sales

Accepted and rejected leads, with reasons, change targeting, forms and scoring. Stations 3 to 5.

{{i:refresh-cw}}
Revenue loop monthly

Qualified-stage events go back to the ad platforms. Unit economics by source move the budget. Stations 5 to 7, back to 1.

The flywheel

Demand that compounds instead of resetting every quarter.

  • Proof earns attention.A trace or a peer story is the only ad worth running in a category nobody is searching for.
  • Attention becomes a conversationwhen the next step is small and the reply is fast.
  • A conversation becomes a pilotwhen success criteria are agreed up front.
  • A pilot becomes a customerwhen the clinician sees it work on their own patients.
  • A customer becomes proof:a story, a quote, a reference call, and with consent, data for the next benchmark.

{{i:info}}The first turn is the expensive one. That is why a quarter of the 90-day budget goes into proof before reach.

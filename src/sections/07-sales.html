7Handing leads to sales

Sales gets leads that meet five tests, and every one of them hears from us within an hour.

What counts as a qualified lead

  1. Works at a target account: a PT group with 5 to 25 locations, or a pro or college sports program, in a region we sell to.
  2. Can approve a purchase or would use the product: an owner or executive, or a clinical or performance lead.
  3. Asked for something: a demo, a pilot or pricing, or replied to outbound.
  4. Named a problem we solve, in their own words: return-to-sport decisions, patients who plateau or drop out, or a new service to sell.
  5. Could start a 30-day pilot within 60 days.

Points 1 to 3 come from the form and Salesforce and put the lead straight on the sales calendar. Sales confirms points 4 and 5 on the call. Leads that miss point 1 or 2 go to the weekly group demo and email instead.

Response times

  • 0 minQualified leads pick a time on a calendar shown right after they submit the form.
  • 15 minFirst call or email if they did not book, during business hours.
  • 1 hrNo lead waits longer than this. Anything older shows in red on the weekly report.
  • 1 daySales accepts or rejects each lead, with a reason from a fixed list.
  • 6 touchesOver ten business days before an unresponsive lead goes back to email nurture.

Salesforce in month one

Week 1

  • Audit fields, the Account Engagement connection and admin access
  • NeuroStrip record types, kept apart from assistive communication
  • Patient records excluded from every marketing list and report
  • Fields for account type, size and role, plus NPI, data source, intent topic, and test or holdout group

Week 2

  • Lifecycle stages, each with a date stamp
  • Lead source and UTM fields that cannot be overwritten
  • "How did you hear about us?" on every form
  • Lead routing with the response-time clock

Week 3

  • Parent accounts for groups, child accounts for clinics
  • Opportunity stages with pilot start, end and success measures
  • Required loss reasons from a fixed list
  • Simple fit and engagement scores

Week 4

  • The weekly report
  • The last 12 months of trials entered with source and outcome
  • Qualified stages sent back to Google Ads
  • Propensity engagement synced to each contact
  • A test lead through every path, and 30 minutes of training for sales

Five numbers reported every week

  1. Qualified discovery calls heldagainst the goal of 20
  2. Leads answered within an houras a share of all qualified leads
  3. Calls that became sales opportunitiesplanned at 55%
  4. Pilots started, and pilots converted to paidplanned at 45% and 50%
  5. Cost per qualified callby channel, against a limit of about $930

Each number is split by channel, by audience and by test half. The $930 limit is a $7,500 first-year contract times the 12% of qualified calls planned to become customers.

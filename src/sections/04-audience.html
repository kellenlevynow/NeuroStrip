What breaks the tie

The scores are close at the top. Four facts are not.

On urgency and on how fast the demo lands, elite sport wins. If the goal were ten famous logos, I would start there. The goal is a repeatable 20 calls a week.

A decision maker you can reach

In a group this size a clinician usually still sits on the leadership team, and the decision maker is within reach.{{ev:s:s15}} Above 50 locations, adoption is measured in quarters. Below five, the contract is small and the default comparison is a $499 biofeedback unit.{{ev:v:s32}}

4

Two hires, two jobs, and a precedent

Two roles are open: business development and demand generation.{{ev:s:s15}} A business development lead is the right tool for a few hundred elite logos. A demand engine is the right tool for 1,000 regional groups. VALD ran the same sequence: elite sport first "because it is high profile", then physiotherapy clinics for scale, where health grew to a quarter of sales.{{ev:v:s38}}

The allocation

70, 20, 10. A sequence, not a split.

70%Volume engine

Regional PT groups

Sports and orthopedic outpatient groups with 5 to 25 locations. Demand gen owns this lane end to end: the account list, both threads into each account, every channel, the pilot path.

  • Named-account list, built and counted in weeks 1 and 2
  • LinkedIn, search and coordinated outbound aimed here first
  • Goal: discovery call, 30-day pilot at one clinic, rollout
20%Proof lane

Elite, pro and college sport

Business development hunts. Demand gen supplies air cover to a short list of programs, the return-to-play content, and the event plan. The logos and stories earned here are the strongest proof the PT lane has.{{ev:h:h9}}

  • Trace and return-to-play assets built once, used in both lanes
  • Title-targeted air cover to named programs
  • Goal: reference stories, not lead volume
10%Capture and route

Everything that arrives

Independent clinics (roughly 22,000 of them{{ev:a:a15}}), performance facilities, neuro rehab and researchers will find us. They get a good landing page, a weekly live group demo and the right nurture track. They do not get outbound, dedicated spend or a 1:1 slot by default.

  • Search and site capture only
  • Group demo before any 1:1 time
  • No patient or consumer advertising

Ideal customer profile

Four tiers, so sales time goes where it compounds.

A

1:1 discovery call

Fast lane. Books straight onto the calendar.

  • Pro and college sports medicine or performance leads
B

Live group demo first

One-to-many, weekly. A 1:1 follows if they engage.

  • Neuro rehab program leads

Useful content, no sales time yet.

  • Researchers without funding

No sales time

Suppressed from routing and audiences.

  • Vendors, competitors, unserviceable regions

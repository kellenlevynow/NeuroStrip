70% of our time and attention

Physical therapy groups with 5 to 25 clinics

A purchased list of about 1,000 groups. Ads, search and email reach their owners, leaders and clinicians. The path is a call, a 30-day pilot at one clinic, then more clinics.

20% of our time and attention

Pro and college sport

Control Bionics' business development lead sells to each team directly. Marketing supplies content on return to sport (getting injured athletes back to play), ads to a short list of named programs and the event plan. The aim is sport customer stories, once cleared, to show physical therapy groups.

10% of our time and attention

Everyone else

Single clinics outside a group, training facilities and researchers find us on their own, through search, the website and a weekly live demo. We send them no emails they did not ask for, and run no ads aimed at patients or the public.

How big the target is

The United States has about 39,000 outpatient physical therapy clinics. About 82% belong to independents and groups with fewer than 25 clinics. About 10,000 clinics sit in groups with 5 to 25 clinics, a planning estimate checked against the purchased list in week one. At an average of 6 to 15 clinics each, that gives roughly 700 to 1,700 groups. The plan uses about 1,000 and counts the real number from the purchased list in week one.3

Why sport gets 20%

Sport teams decide fast and know muscle testing well, so the demo lands quickly. But there are too few of them to fill 20 calls a week, budgets reset once a year, and two roles are open at Control Bionics for exactly this split: business development for named sport programs, and demand generation for volume. Sport stories then help sell to physical therapy groups.

What would change the order

If the week-one count shows far fewer than 700 groups, we add groups with 26 to 50 clinics before adding new audiences. If Propensity matches too little of the list in week 3, we test PulsePoint or DeepIntent. If fewer than 60% of groups are matched by Day 30, the ad budget shifts to search, email and the conference, and we add groups with 26 to 50 clinics to the list.

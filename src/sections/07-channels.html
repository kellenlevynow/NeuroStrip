Budget

Only about half of the first 90 days is media. That is the point.

A quarter buys the proof that makes ads worth running. An eighth buys the measurement that tells us which ads to keep. The budget was not disclosed, so the percentages are the recommendation and the dollars scale.{{ev:a:a2}}

90-day program budget $90,000

Excludes salaries, event costs and the existing outbound retainer.

{{i:info}}Media and channel costs make up 55% of the total. Inside that 55%, the split is LinkedIn 45, search 20, email 10, retargeting 10 and capped bets 15. The 8% reserve is released at the Day 30 and Day 60 reviews to whatever is winning.

Scenario

Stretch keeps media spend the same and assumes three things go right: outbound is warmed and widened, a second weekly demo fills, and event follow-up lands.

{{i:info}}Benchmarks behind the defaults: LinkedIn cost per lead averages $276 and runs $400 to $600 in healthcare technology{{ev:v:s29}}; search averages $5.42 a click and an 8.2% click-to-lead rate across industries{{ev:v:s30}}; cold email averages a 3.4% reply rate, 5.5% in the top quartile{{ev:v:s31}}. Funnel rates and contract value are placeholders until the trial backfill.{{ev:a:a6,a7}} Calls booked near Day 90 become customers around Day 150, after a 30-day pilot.

Lever 1

Outbound, warmed and widened

In the stretch case outbound doubles from 2.7 to 5.4 calls a week at no extra media cost: warmed accounts hold about a third more calls per 100 contacts{{ev:h:h4}}, and the sequence widens from 300 to 450 contacts a week. On direct response alone LinkedIn runs at about twice the allowable cost per call, so this lift is how it earns its budget. The holdout test tells us by Day 60.

Lever 2

A second weekly live demo

One-to-many time scales without touching the inside-sales calendar. A second slot aimed at Tier A clinic directors is the cheapest capacity in the plan. In the stretch case registrants rise from 60 to 90 a month and 18% book, which moves demos from 2.1 to 3.7 calls a week.

Lever 3

The event follow-up

With a December start, APTA CSM lands in the scale phase.{{ev:v:s26}} Pre-booked meetings, a booth test people queue for and a 48-hour follow-up rule turn one week into a month of calls.{{ev:h:h6}} In the stretch case 200 qualified conversations a quarter and a 22% booking rate move events from 2.3 to 3.4 calls a week.

The guardrail that replaces cost per lead

Allowable cost per held call=first-year contract value×call-to-customer rate

  • $433at a $3,500 contract

Every channel is judged against this figure, at the call-to-customer rate in the model above.{{ev:a:a6,a7}} The stated focus is on building the customer base quickly.{{ev:v:s8}} On that footing, paying up to a full first-year contract to win an account that then expands is a defensible trade. How much tolerance to allow is leadership's call, and it is one slider in the model.

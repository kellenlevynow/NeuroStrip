Learn more about the account-based ad test

The test runs for 90 days on 45% of the ad and email money, $22,500 at the base budget. The owner of every step is the demand generation lead, with leadership deciding at Day 30 and Day 60.

StepWhat happens
Build the list
Weeks 1 and 2		We buy a list from a healthcare provider database, HealthLink Dimensions or CampaignLake. Both use records. We keep physical therapy practices in the states Control Bionics sells into, and the seven titles in the buying group. Neither site shows a filter for number of clinics, so we keep groups with 5 to 25 clinics by counting each group's practice addresses. CampaignLake lists a physical therapist segment and HealthLink Dimensions does not name one, so we check both free samples in week one.
Flag groups researching now
Weekly from week 2		Bombora shows which groups are reading more than usual about topics such as physical therapy billing software, electronic medical record (EMR) migration, rehab equipment and biofeedback. Those groups get ads and email first. Bombora reports by company and does not name people. The exact topic names are checked against Bombora's topic list in week one.
Run the ads
From week 4		Propensity runs the ads. It describes itself as a contact-level business-to-business marketing platform. It builds audiences by industry, job role, tech stack and behavior, runs ads on LinkedIn, Meta, the Propensity Display Network, native and contextual display, direct mail and geofencing, and sends contact-level engagement into Salesforce. We upload our own list to it. Its pricing page (propensity.com/pricing, viewed October 2026) lists the plan that includes LinkedIn, Meta and direct mail at $3,000 a month, before ad spend. Which plan we need is confirmed in the week-one demo.
Split the list
Week 3		Groups are split at random into two halves of about 500. The test half gets ads plus email. The gets email only. The split is made separately for groups researching now and the rest, so both halves have the same mix.
Day 30 check
December 30, 2026		At least 60% of target groups must match to a named, verified decision maker. If not, the ad money moves to search, email and the APTA Combined Sections Meeting (CSM), and we add groups with 26 to 50 clinics to the list.
Day 60 check
January 29, 2027		The ads stop only if the test half is behind the holdout half on both interested replies and qualified calls per 100 groups. If it is ahead on both, the month-three increase goes ahead. If it is level, or ahead on only one, spend stays at the month-two level until Day 90.
Day 90 check
Review on February 26, 2027		The ads keep their money next quarter only if the test half books at least 20% more qualified calls per 100 groups than the holdout half, at under $930 a call. If not, the ad money moves to search and email. The demand generation lead brings the numbers, and leadership decides.
Month three
From January 30, 2027		Ad spend moves to groups researching now. A runs around APTA CSM in Philadelphia, February 4 to 6, 2027.
Fallback
Decided in week 3		By December 21, if Propensity matches fewer than 60% of groups to a named decision maker, the same list runs through a healthcare ad platform that targets by NPI, PulsePoint or DeepIntent, and the test moves to whichever matches more. The Day 30 check then applies to the platform in use. Both target and report on individual providers by NPI and accept an uploaded NPI list. They reach owners and clinic directors who hold an NPI, but not executives without one. Neither site names physical therapists, so our list gets a match test first. Neither publishes prices, so both are quotes in week one.

How sure the Day 60 read can be

Email starts in week 5, so by Day 60 it has reached about 1,350 contacts, about 675 in each half, many still partway through the four emails. At the planned 1.5% that is about 10 interested replies a half, so a 20% lift is about 2 replies, which chance alone can produce. The Day 60 read checks that nothing is broken and that the ads are not clearly worse. The full read comes at Day 90, once most of the list has been emailed, using replies and qualified calls per 100 groups together.

The APTA Combined Sections Meeting in February

The American Physical Therapy Association's Combined Sections Meeting (APTA CSM) runs February 4 to 6, 2027, in Philadelphia. We book meetings with target practices before the show, run the geofence and a 60-second muscle test at the booth during it, and follow up every conversation within 48 hours. Event costs sit in their own budget.

What we are holding back on

LinkedIn self-serve prospecting, which reaches this buyer poorly. Also broad Meta prospecting beyond the verified list, paid YouTube reach, content syndication, and any patient or consumer advertising.

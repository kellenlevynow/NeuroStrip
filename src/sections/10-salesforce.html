10Salesforce, month one {{i:circle-help}}Answers question 5

Wiring first. Dashboards second.

Month one builds the smallest structure that can answer one question: which audiences, messages, channels and campaigns produce revenue? Every item below captures that information, protects it, or reports it. Nothing else gets built yet.

{{i:search}}

A finding from the public site

The demo request forms on controlbionics.com post to an address in the pattern Salesforce Account Engagement uses, on a form dated January 2022.{{ev:v:s5}} So an engagement layer appears to have been in place for years. Month one is about wiring it to NeuroStrip, not buying something new. Editions, connector status and admin access get confirmed in the first week.{{ev:a:a10}}

Week 1

Audit and separate

  • Audit what existsObjects, fields, picklists, the connector, who has admin rights. Write a one-page data dictionary.
  • NeuroStrip record typesIts own lead, account and opportunity layouts and sales process, so reports never mix two businesses.
  • Wall off patient recordsAssistive communication records are excluded from every marketing audience and report.
  • Segment, tier and persona fieldsWithout them there is no "by audience" in any report.
Week 2

Capture and route

  • Lifecycle stage, with a timestamp per stageNo timestamps, no velocity, no conversion rates.
  • Source that survivesLocked lead-source picklist, first and last touch UTMs, click IDs, and "how did you hear about us?" on every hand-raise form.
  • Campaign hierarchyPeriod, audience, message, channel, asset. It mirrors the reporting question.
  • Routing and the SLA clockAssignment rules, instant scheduling, a timer on first touch, required reject reasons.
Week 3

Pipeline and scoring

  • Account structureParent and child accounts for groups, number of locations, lead-to-account matching, buying-group roles.
  • Opportunity stages with pilot trackingExit criteria, required primary campaign, pilot dates, success criteria, the five PILOT fields.
  • Loss reasons from a fixed list"Other" teaches nothing.
  • Fit grade and engagement score, version oneNative grading and scoring, deliberately simple, reviewed with sales monthly.
Week 4

Report and close the loop

  • Five dashboardsCalendar, funnel and velocity, speed to lead, source to revenue, pilot board.
  • Backfill the trial baseTwelve months of trials entered with segment, source, outcome and reason.
  • Send qualified stages back to the ad platformsSo they optimize toward sales-accepted calls, not form fills.{{ev:v:s40}}{{ev:h:h12}}
  • End-to-end test and trainingA test lead through every path. A 30-minute walkthrough with sales.

Naming convention

If the link is named properly, the report writes itself.

Every paid and owned link carries the same five tags in the same order. Build one.

The campaign tag is the same string as the Salesforce campaign name, so ad platform data and CRM data join without a lookup table.

Reporting

Five dashboards. Each answers one question.

The calendar

"Are we on pace for 20?"

Calls booked and held each week against target, by segment and source, with no-show rate.

Funnel and velocity

"Where do leads stall?"

Stage-to-stage conversion and days in stage, by tier and segment.

Speed to lead

"Are we answering fast enough?"

Median time to first touch, share inside the SLA, untouched MQLs by age.

Source to revenue

"What is producing pipeline?"

Pipeline and revenue by audience, message, channel and campaign, with cost per accepted call, per pilot and per customer.

Pilot board

"Which pilots will convert?"

Active pilots, days in, status against criteria, and how much of the kit pool is out.

{{i:rocket}}

The head start: backfill the trials

Control Bionics has been running trials for about a year, with organizations across the US and Australia.{{ev:s:s15}}{{ev:v:s9}} Entering each one with segment, source, outcome and reason gives real trial-to-paid and cycle-time baselines on Day 30 instead of Day 180, plus a seed list for lookalike audiences. It is the cheapest data the company will ever buy.

{{i:ban}}

What does not get built in month one

  • Weighted multi-touch attribution. First touch, last touch and self-reported source are enough to start.
  • Predictive scoring. There is not yet enough closed data to train it.
  • Long qualifying forms. Four fields and a calendar.
  • Branching nurture trees. Three short tracks by persona.

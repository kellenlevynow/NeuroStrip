6The first 90 days

We spend month one setting up tracking and content, month two testing messages and offers, and month three putting money behind what worked.

Dates assume Day 1 is December 1, 2026. Full launch then lands in the first week of January, after the holidays, and APTA CSM falls in week 10.

Days 1 to 30

Set up

  1. Week 1Vendor demos and coverage counts. Propensity, HealthLink Dimensions, CampaignLake and Bombora each count the PT practices with 5 to 25 locations they cover in our states. Confirm email and ad use rights before signing. Listen to ten sales calls, interview trial customers, and agree the qualified-lead checklist with sales.
  2. Week 2Buy the list and load it into Salesforce with NPI, data source and intent fields. Salesforce stages, source tracking and lead routing go live. Launch Google Search. Draft the approved claims list with regulatory and book two customer video shoots.
  3. Week 3Build the Propensity audience, then split target accounts at random into a test half and a holdout half. If Propensity matches too little of the list, move the test to PulsePoint or DeepIntent. Build the four landing pages, produce the 90-second video and a pilot guide, and re-brief the outbound partner.
  4. Week 4Launch the account-based ads to the test half at low spend. Run the first weekly live demo. The weekly report goes live, and a test lead runs through every path.

Day 30 checkpoint

6 to 8 qualified calls a week

At least 60% of target accounts match to a verified decision maker, or the account-based money moves to search, email and CSM. Decide: pick the two messages to test and approve month-two spend.

Days 31 to 60

Test

  1. Week 5Full launch. Outbound starts to both halves, in-market practices first, and two messages are tested against two buyer types.
  2. Week 6Make the first cut of ads that are not working. Add new keywords and exclusions from the search terms report. Open meeting bookings for CSM.
  3. Week 7Test the offers: pilot scoping call against live demo. Publish the first customer story.
  4. Week 8Cut the bottom half of ads and audiences. Choose the winning message and offer.

Day 60 checkpoint

10 to 12 qualified calls a week

The test half replies to outbound at least 20% more often than the holdout half, or books more qualified calls per 100 accounts. If neither, the ads stop. Decide: release the reserve to what is working.

Days 61 to 90

Scale

  1. Week 9Move ad spend to in-market practices. Raise spend on what works by up to 25% a week. Widen outbound if reply rates hold.
  2. Week 10APTA CSM runs February 4 to 6 in Philadelphia, with a geofence around the convention center. Hold the pre-booked meetings, run the booth muscle test, and get every badge scan into Salesforce the same day.
  3. Week 11Follow up every CSM conversation within 48 hours. Publish the second customer story. First newsletter sponsorship goes live.
  4. Weeks 12 and 13Read the first pilot results against their success measures. Hold the Day 90 review of cost per call, per pilot and per customer, by channel, audience and test half.

Day 90 checkpoint

15 to 20 qualified calls a week

About 130 qualified calls held in total and 30 pilots started or scheduled. Decide: next quarter's budget, whether to renew the 90-day data and ABM contracts, a second sales seat, and the second audience to target.

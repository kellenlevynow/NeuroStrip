6The first 90 days

We spend month one setting up tracking and content, month two testing messages and offers, and month three putting money behind what worked.

Dates assume Day 1 is December 1, 2026. Full launch then lands in the first week of January, after the holidays, and APTA CSM falls in week 10.

Days 1 to 30

Set up

  1. Week 1Listen to ten sales calls and interview eight to ten trial customers. Audit Salesforce, the website and tracking. Agree the qualified-lead checklist and response times with sales. Draft the approved claims list with regulatory.
  2. Week 2Salesforce stages, source tracking and lead routing go live. Fix the website's demo request path. Launch Google Search. Book two customer video shoots.
  3. Week 3Produce the 90-second demo video, two customer stories and a pilot guide. Enter the last 12 months of trials in Salesforce. Load the target account list into LinkedIn. Re-brief the outbound partner.
  4. Week 4LinkedIn goes live at low spend. Run the first weekly live demo. The weekly report goes live, and a test lead runs through every path.

Day 30 checkpoint

6 to 8 qualified calls a week

Every new lead carries its source and audience. Decide: confirm the account list, pick the two messages to test and approve month-two spend.

Days 31 to 60

Test

  1. Week 5Full launch, with two messages tested against two buyer types. Outbound sequences start, with a held-out group of accounts that do not see LinkedIn ads.
  2. Week 6Make the first cut of ads that are not working. Add new keywords and exclusions from the search terms report. Open meeting bookings for CSM.
  3. Week 7Test the offers: pilot scoping call against live demo. Publish the first customer story.
  4. Week 8Cut the bottom half of ads and audiences. Choose the winning message and offer.

Day 60 checkpoint

10 to 12 qualified calls a week

Half of target accounts have seen the ads three or more times. Decide: release the reserve to what is working and decide on a Meta test.

Days 61 to 90

Scale

  1. Week 9Raise spend on what works by up to 25% a week. Widen outbound if reply rates hold.
  2. Week 10APTA CSM runs February 4 to 6. Hold the pre-booked meetings, run the booth muscle test, and get every badge scan into Salesforce the same day.
  3. Week 11Follow up every CSM conversation within 48 hours. Publish the second customer story. First newsletter sponsorship goes live.
  4. Weeks 12 and 13Send qualified stages back to LinkedIn and Google. Read the first pilot results against their success measures. Hold the Day 90 review of cost per call, per pilot and per customer, by channel and audience.

Day 90 checkpoint

15 to 20 qualified calls a week

About 130 qualified calls held in total and 30 pilots started or scheduled. Decide: next quarter's budget, a second sales seat, and the second audience to target.

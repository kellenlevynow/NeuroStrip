What this means for the plan

  • Ready to sell. Cleared in three markets, with signed customers. The gap is that buyers do not know the category exists.
  • Proof is real, and thin in public. Two PT groups are named and one case study is still in progress. So a quarter of the budget buys proof first.
  • The stack looks to be there. Month one wires it to NeuroStrip instead of buying something new.
  • A clean start. Nothing is running, so there is no baseline to protect. The trial base becomes the baseline.

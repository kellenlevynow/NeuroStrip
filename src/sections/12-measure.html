12Measurement

Three levels. Only the top two are goals.

Marketing indicators are instruments on the dashboard, not the destination. They tell us where to look. They never tell us whether it is working. A falling cost per lead is a warning until the sales-accepted rate confirms it.

Level 1

Business outcomes

What leadership is paying for
  • New customers
  • First-year revenue
  • Qualified pipeline
  • Customer acquisition cost
  • Payback period
  • Expansion: sites live per account
Level 2

Funnel outcomes

What this role is accountable for
  • Held, qualified calls a week
  • Sales-accepted rate
  • SQL rate
  • Pilot starts
  • Pilot to paid
  • Cycle length by stage
  • Cost per held call, per pilot, per customer
Level 3

Marketing indicators

Diagnostics only. Never a target.
  • Tier A account reach and engagement
  • Click-through rate
  • Cost per click
  • Cost per lead
  • Landing-page conversion
  • Reply rate
  • Retargeting pool size

Attribution

Three lenses, read together. None is trusted alone.

{{i:link}}

What the click says

UTMs and click IDs on first and last touch. Precise, and blind to everything that happens off a link: a colleague's mention, a podcast, a booth conversation.

{{i:message-square}}

What the buyer says

"How did you hear about us?" as an open field on every hand-raise form, read weekly. It catches the word of mouth that clinical markets run on.

{{i:split}}

What the holdout says

Account-level tests: some Tier A accounts get the air cover and some do not. In a market of 1,000 accounts this is the only clean read on what a channel caused.

05Positioning and awareness

Force shows what the body produced. NeuroStrip shows which muscle produced it.

NeuroStrip does not have an awareness problem. It has a category problem.{{ev:h:h1}} The people who should buy it already believe they measure readiness, so nobody is searching for what it does. The message has to open a gap before it can offer to close it.

What they believe

"We already test for this."

Patients plateau or stop coming. Athletes re-injure. Reimbursement is squeezed and it is hard to stand out. But strength and function get tested, so readiness is covered.

{{i:arrow-right}}
What they do not see yet

Output hides compensation.

Force plates, dynamometers and hop tests measure what the limb produced. A limb can pass while the injured muscle is still under-recruiting and its neighbors cover for it.{{ev:v:s18}}

{{i:arrow-right}}
What changes their mind

Seeing it on a screen.

Two graphs, one patient, one minute. The demonstration is the argument. Everything upstream exists to earn that minute.{{ev:h:h5}}

Positioning statement

For clinicians and performance staff who decide when someone is ready, NeuroStrip is the wearable activation sensor that shows which muscles are doing the work, live, in the clinic or on the field.

Unlike force plates and dynamometers, which measure output, or lab EMG, which needs a lab, it goes on like a bandage and puts activation next to the measures you already trust.

Claims guardrail

NeuroStrip is registered as a non-diagnostic measurement and biofeedback tool.{{ev:v:s8}} Copy stays inside that line, and a claims matrix is approved with regulatory before anything ships. Reimbursement language gets the same care: Medicare covers biofeedback only for specific indications.{{ev:v:s35}}

Say freely

  • Measures and shows muscle activation
  • Live biofeedback
  • Objective data to inform a decision
  • Four muscles at once, no wires

Say with a source

  • A named customer's outcome, with consent
  • Published research, cited
  • Program economics, with the clinic's own numbers

Never say

  • Diagnoses or predicts
  • Prevents injury or re-injury
  • Certainty, guarantee, proven to
  • Billable, without compliance sign-off

Awareness framework

Seven stages. One rule: nobody at stage one or two ever sees a demo ad.

A sophisticated product advertised to people who do not know they have the problem buys clicks and nothing else. Each stage gets its own message, format, call to action and measure.

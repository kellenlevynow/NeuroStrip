Team, asks and risks

Strategy and data stay with Control Bionics. Partners do the specialist work.

We ask for every data and ad contract in Control Bionics' name with a 90-day term, so Control Bionics keeps the data and can stop or switch any partner at the Day 90 review. Each term is confirmed in the vendor's week-one quote. To start on real numbers, we need fourteen answers in week one.

Stays in-house

  • Strategy, the target list and the buying group playbook
  • Messages, offers and the list of what we may say about NeuroStrip, agreed with the regulatory team
  • Test design and every decision to cut or scale
  • Ad accounts, tracking and data, always in Control Bionics' name
  • Day-to-day ad spending and the weekly report

Goes to partners

  • The provider list: HealthLink Dimensions or CampaignLake
  • Research data: Bombora
  • Account-based ads: Propensity, with PulsePoint or DeepIntent as the fallback
  • Video shoots and design: a studio or freelancers, per project
  • Email sending: the email agency, which already sends Control Bionics' first emails to new contacts, with a new brief
  • Salesforce build: Control Bionics' Salesforce admin, or a certified consultant for four weeks if the week-one answers show no admin time. The demand generation lead decides by December 4 and owns the build.

Fourteen answers we need in week one.

Each one replaces a planning figure or settles something sales will be asked. Control Bionics names one person to answer each ask by December 7. Anything still open then stays marked confirm in week one, and the pieces that depend on it, such as the mailed box and the pricing email, wait.

  1. The budget. The 90-day number and what it covers. The plan assumes $90,000, before salaries, events and the email agency's monthly fee. It also sets the email agency's sending plan of 300 new contacts a week.
  2. Salesforce and ad accounts. Admin access to Salesforce, Google Ads and Meta. Propensity's site says Meta ads need an ad account with at least 60 days of history (propensity.com, viewed October 2026). If Control Bionics' Meta account is newer, Meta ads through Propensity cannot start in week 4.
  3. Sales capacity. How many calls Tanner's calendar can hold, assumed at about 20 a week, and who covers when it is full.
  4. Pilot kits and terms. How many kits are available to lend each month (the Base budget plans about 16 pilots a month by Day 90), and the standard pilot terms. Confirm in week one.
  5. History. Every trial from the last 12 months with where it came from, how it ended and what the contract was worth, plus first sales calls by where the lead came from for the last eight weeks. This replaces the estimate of about 4 qualified calls a week that already come in. Also the count of good conversations from the last conference, which replaces the planned 150.
  6. Where we sell. The states Control Bionics sells into, which decide which groups go on the purchased list.
  7. Medical record systems. Whether NeuroStrip connects to the clinic's electronic medical record (EMR) today, and which ones. Confirm in week one.
  8. Where patient data goes. Where session data is stored and who can see it, in writing for practice administrators. Confirm in week one.
  9. Support. What setup, training and support a new clinic gets, and how fast. Confirm in week one.
  10. Billing. What current customers do about billing, so sales can describe it without making promises. The clinic's billing team always decides.
  11. Price. The annual price for one clinic, the price for each extra clinic on the same contract, what the price includes and the cost of replacement sensors. Confirm in week one.
  12. Margin. What Control Bionics keeps from a clinic contract after product and support costs, so payback counts real profit.
  13. Cleared customers. Which customer names, logos and results we may use, and in which channels.
  14. Time with sales and customers. Ten recorded sales calls and eight to ten customers from past trials to interview.

Rules for every partner

  • No media agency this quarter. Ads and email peak at about $22,500 in month three at Base, which the demand generation lead runs in-house. Leadership revisits this at Day 90.
  • Every partner works in Control Bionics' accounts and is judged on qualified calls.
  • Partners are paid fixed fees. No partner is paid a share of ad spend.
  • We ask for 90-day terms on data and ad contracts, confirmed in each quote. Other partners can be ended on 30 days' notice.
  • Propensity publishes its prices. The list, intent and fallback vendors do not, so each is a quote in week one.

Team, requests and risks

Control Bionics retains strategy and data, and partners deliver specialist work.

We will request every data and advertising contract in Control Bionics' name with a 90-day term, so Control Bionics owns the data and can stop or replace any partner at the Day 90 review. Each vendor confirms its term in its week-one quote. To plan on actual figures, we need fourteen answers in week one.

Retained in-house

  • Strategy, the target account list and the buying group playbook
  • Messaging, offers and the list of approved claims about NeuroStrip, agreed with the regulatory team
  • Test design and every decision to cut or scale
  • Ad accounts, tracking and data, always held in Control Bionics' name
  • Day-to-day management of ad spend and the weekly report

Assigned to partners

  • Provider list: HealthLink Dimensions or CampaignLake
  • Research data: Bombora
  • Account-based ads: Propensity, with PulsePoint or DeepIntent as the fallback
  • Video production and design: a studio or freelancers, engaged per project
  • Email sending: the email agency that already sends Control Bionics' first emails to new contacts, working from a new brief
  • Salesforce build: Control Bionics' Salesforce admin, or a certified consultant for four weeks if the week-one answers show no admin time. The demand generation lead decides by December 4 and owns the build.

We need fourteen answers from Control Bionics in week one.

Each answer replaces a planning figure or settles a question sales will face. Control Bionics names one owner for each request by December 7. Any item still open on that date remains marked as confirm in week one, and the deliverables that depend on it, such as the mailed box and the pricing email, wait.

  1. The budget. Confirm the 90-day budget and what it covers. The plan assumes $90,000, excluding salaries, events and the email agency's monthly fee. The budget also sets the email agency's sending plan of 300 new contacts a week.
  2. Salesforce and ad accounts. Grant admin access to Salesforce, Google Ads and Meta. Propensity's site states that Meta ads require an ad account with at least 60 days of history (propensity.com, viewed October 2026). If Control Bionics' Meta account is newer, Meta ads through Propensity cannot start in week 4.
  3. Sales capacity. Confirm how many calls Tanner's calendar can hold, assumed at about 20 a week, and who covers when it is full.
  4. Pilot kits and terms. Confirm how many kits are available to lend each month and the standard pilot terms. The Base budget plans about 16 pilots a month by Day 90. Confirm in week one.
  5. History. Provide every trial from the last 12 months with its source, outcome and contract value, plus first sales calls by lead source for the last eight weeks. This data replaces the estimate of about 4 qualified calls a week that already come in. Also provide the count of productive conversations from the last conference, which replaces the planned 150.
  6. Sales territory. List the states Control Bionics sells into, which determine which groups go on the purchased list.
  7. Medical record systems. Confirm whether NeuroStrip connects to the clinic's electronic medical record (EMR) today, and to which systems. Confirm in week one.
  8. Patient data handling. Document where session data is stored and who can access it, in writing for practice administrators. Confirm in week one.
  9. Support. Describe the setup, training and support a new clinic receives, and how quickly each is delivered. Confirm in week one.
  10. Billing. Explain how current customers handle billing, so sales can describe it without making promises. The clinic's billing team always makes the decision.
  11. Price. Provide the annual price for one clinic, the price for each additional clinic on the same contract, what the price includes and the cost of replacement sensors. Confirm in week one.
  12. Margin. State what Control Bionics retains from a clinic contract after product and support costs, so payback calculations reflect actual profit.
  13. Cleared customers. Identify which customer names, logos and results we may use, and in which channels.
  14. Time with sales and customers. Share ten recorded sales calls and arrange interviews with eight to ten customers from past trials.

Vendor management rules for every partner

  • Control Bionics engages no media agency this quarter. Ads and email peak at about $22,500 in month three at Base, which the demand generation lead manages in-house. Leadership revisits this decision at Day 90.
  • Every partner works in Control Bionics' accounts and is evaluated on qualified calls.
  • Partners are paid fixed fees. No partner is paid a share of ad spend.
  • We request 90-day terms on data and ad contracts, confirmed in each quote. Other partners can be ended on 30 days' notice.
  • Propensity publishes its prices. The list, intent and fallback vendors do not, so each one provides a quote in week one.

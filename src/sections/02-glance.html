Control Bionics asked five questions. Here are the short answers.

Which audience comes first?
Physical therapy groups with 5 to 25 clinics get 70% of the team's time and attention. Pro and college sport gets 20%, sold team by team by Control Bionics' business development lead. Everyone else gets 10%: they find us through search, the website and a weekly live demo.
Which channels, and how is the budget split?
to half of a purchased list of about 1,000 groups get 45% of the $49,500 for ads and email. Google Search gets 20%, email 11%, 9% and small tests 15%.
What goes to an agency, and what stays in-house?
Strategy, messages and spending decisions stay with Control Bionics. Agencies and vendors supply the list, the research data, the ads, video, email sending and four weeks of setup in . Every data and ad contract is in Control Bionics' name and runs for 90 days.
What is a qualified lead, and how is it measured?
We call it a : a leader or clinician at a target group who asked for a demo, a pilot or pricing. Their first call counts as one qualified call once they name a problem NeuroStrip helps with and could start a pilot within 60 days. We follow each one to a pilot and a signed contract, and report the cost of each step.
What gets built in Salesforce in month one?
NeuroStrip leads kept apart from Control Bionics' communication devices business, a date on every step from call to contract, a record of where each lead came from that nobody can change, boxes for role, message and objection, a one-hour reply rule, pilot tracking and one weekly report.

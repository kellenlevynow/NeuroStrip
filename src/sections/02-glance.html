The plan at a glance

A $90,000 investment over 90 days secures three quarters of the target call volume.

The goal is 20 qualified calls a week. Starting from Day 1 on December 1, 2026, the plan reaches about 15 a week by Day 90. Reaching 20 depends on the Day 90 decisions on budget and list size. Attribution traces every call to its source.

Control Bionics posed five questions. The short answers follow.

Which audience comes first?
Physical therapy groups with 5 to 25 clinics receive 70% of the team's time and attention. Professional and college sport receives 20%, sold team by team by Control Bionics' business development lead. All other segments receive 10% and reach us through search, the website and a weekly live demo.
Which channels, and how is the budget split?
to half of a purchased list of about 1,000 groups receive 45% of the $49,500 paid media and email budget. Google Search receives 20%, email 11%, 9% and small tests 15%.
What goes to an agency, and what stays in-house?
Strategy, messaging and budget decisions stay with Control Bionics. Agencies and vendors supply the list, the research data, ad production, video, email delivery and four weeks of setup in . We request every data and advertising contract in Control Bionics' name with a 90-day term, to be confirmed in each vendor's week-one quote.
What is a qualified lead, and how is it measured?
We define it as a : a leader or clinician at a target group who has requested a demo, a pilot or pricing. That person's first call counts as one qualified call once they name a problem NeuroStrip addresses and could start a pilot within 60 days. We track each lead through pilot to signed contract and report the cost at each stage.
What is built in Salesforce in month one?
The month-one build separates NeuroStrip leads from Control Bionics' communication devices business and records a date for every step from call to contract. It also creates a locked record of where each lead came from, pick lists for persona (job title), buying role, message variant (which message the lead received) and objection heard, a one-hour reply rule, pilot tracking and one weekly report.

08Creative engine

A creative engine, not a campaign.

I started with fifteen candidate territories. They collapse into six worth running and five worth parking for now. Every piece that runs is attached to a hypothesis, a spend cap and a rule for what happens when the result comes in.

{{i:ban}}Five territories parked for the first 90 days, and why{{i:chevron-down}}

    How creative evolves

    Hypothesis, creative, audience, channel, result, learning, next hypothesis.

    One variable at a time. Each round narrows the next. This is the first branch of the tree, written before any result exists.

    Round 1 · weeks 5 and 6

    Which idea opens the door?

    Hypothesis
    PT executives respond to the clinical gap more than to the retention story.{{ev:h:h5}}
    Creative
    A split-screen trace. B "Patients don't quit therapy."
    Audience
    Owners and COOs at Tier A accounts.
    Channel
    LinkedIn, equal spend.
    Read at
    $1,500 an arm or 14 days. Metric: booked calls from Tier A, then engaged accounts.
    Wins if
    30% or more ahead on the metric, with no drop in the share of leads from Tier A and B.
    Floor
    Click-through of 0.35% and at least one Tier A booked call. An arm under the floor does not count as a result.

    If A wins · round 2

    Who should say it?

    Hold the trace. Test a clinician's voice against the company page, then a 20-second cut against the 90-second one.

    {{i:arrow-right}}Round 3: which offer follows the trace: live demo or pilot call.

    If B wins · round 2

    Which number lands?

    Hold the retention story. Test the economics model against a peer group's rollout story as the proof.

    {{i:arrow-right}}Round 3: move the trace to the clinician thread only and re-test.

    If neither clears the floor

    The problem is upstream.

    Check the audience before the message: match rates, titles, frequency. Then re-read the week-one interviews for language we missed.

    {{i:arrow-right}}No new creative until the audience is confirmed.

    Proof sprint · weeks 2 to 4

    Six assets feed every channel.

    This is where a quarter of the budget goes. Without these, the ads have nothing to point at and the calls have nothing to send afterward.

    • {{i:video}}The 90-second traceMotion explainer of force against activation. Cut to 20 seconds for stage 1.
    • {{i:camera}}Two customer storiesShot on site at trial customers, with consent. One clinician voice, one owner voice.
    • {{i:file-text}}The field guideWhat strength tests miss, with the published evidence. Runs as a document ad and a download.
    • {{i:circle-dollar-sign}}Clinic economics modelThe visitor's own numbers in, a one-page result out. The executive thread's main offer.
    • {{i:list-checks}}The pilot blueprintThirty days, one clinic, three clinicians, agreed success criteria. One page.
    • {{i:presentation}}Champion kitWhat a clinician hands leadership: the case, the cost, the rollout plan, the data FAQ.

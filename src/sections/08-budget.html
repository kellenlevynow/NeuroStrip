How qualified calls are forecast

Each source has a planned weekly rate at Day 90. Only Google Search and the account-based ads scale with the budget: their month-three spend is divided by a planned cost per qualified call. All other sources are held constant, which is why the range between Lean and Aggressive is narrow.

Each call is counted once and attributed to the source Salesforce records first. The demo and conference rates count only people who did not first reply to email or an ad.

How qualified calls convert to customers

Of every 100 qualified calls, about 55 become real sales opportunities, about 25 start a pilot and about 12 sign. With a first-year value of $7,500, the most a qualified call can cost and still be covered by first-year payments is about $930. That ceiling is calculated before product, sales and pilot kit costs.

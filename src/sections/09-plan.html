Days 1 to 30 · December

Set up

Day 30 check, December 30. 6 to 8 qualified calls a week, and Propensity has found a named decision maker at 60% or more of the target groups. If it finds fewer, the ads would reach too few groups, so the ad money moves to search, email and the conference. Leadership decides.

  1. Week 1 · December 1 to 7

    Demos from four suppliers: Propensity (runs the account-based ads), HealthLink Dimensions and CampaignLake (sell provider lists) and Bombora (shows which groups are researching now). Each counts the groups with 5 to 25 clinics it covers in our states. Confirm in writing that the list can be used for email and ads. Listen to ten sales calls, interview customers from past trials, and agree the five-point checklist with sales.

    Owner: the demand generation lead (the marketer who runs this plan), with sales

  2. Week 2 · December 8 to 14

    Buy the list and load it into Salesforce with each group's NPI, which list it came from, what Bombora says it is researching, and each person's role. The steps a lead moves through, the record of where each lead came from and the rule that sends new leads to sales go live. Launch Google Search. Draft, with the regulatory team, the list of what we may and may not say.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the Salesforce partner and regulatory

  3. Week 3 · December 15 to 21

    Build the Propensity audience and split the groups at random into the test half and the holdout half. If Propensity matches too little of the list, switch to PulsePoint or DeepIntent. Build the four landing pages, the 90-second video and the pilot guide, and brief the agency that sends our first-contact emails.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the production studio

  4. Week 4 · December 22 to 28

    Launch the account-based ads to the test half at low spend over the holidays. Run the first weekly live demo. The weekly report goes live, and a pretend lead goes through every form, email and calendar link to check each step works.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

Days 31 to 60 · January

Test

Day 60 check, January 29. 10 to 12 qualified calls a week. The half that sees ads must reply to email at least 20% more often than the holdout half, or book more qualified calls per 100 groups. If neither, the ads stop. Leadership decides, and the reserve goes to what is working.

  1. Week 5 · December 29 to January 4

    Full launch. Email starts to both halves, groups researching now first. The persona tests begin: message A against message B in the executive, operations and clinical clusters. We also test whether contacting a group's clinical leaders first, or its executives first, books more calls.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the outbound email partner

  2. Week 6 · January 5 to 11

    First cut of ads that are not working. Read Google's report of what people actually typed, add the good searches as keywords and block the ones that waste money. Start booking meetings for the conference.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  3. Week 7 · January 12 to 18

    Test which offer books more calls: a call to plan a pilot, or a live demo. Publish the first customer story, if a customer has cleared it.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  4. Week 8 · January 19 to 25

    Read the persona tests once each message has reached about 150 people. If one message's rate of interested replies is at least 30% higher, that cluster keeps it. A winner with fewer than 10 interested replies is used for now and tested again in month four. Clusters where neither message is 30% ahead keep testing for up to six weeks. Cut the bottom half of ads and audiences.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, reviewed with sales

Days 61 to 90 · February

Scale

Day 90 check, February 28. About 15 qualified calls a week, about 130 held in total and about 30 pilots started or scheduled. Leadership decides next quarter's budget, whether to renew the 90-day data and ad contracts, and whether to hire a second inside salesperson alongside Tanner.

  1. Week 9 · January 26 to February 1

    Move ad spend to groups researching now. Raise spend on what works by up to 25% a week. Remaining persona tests close by the end of week 10.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  2. Week 10 · February 2 to 8

    APTA CSM, February 4 to 6 in Philadelphia, with phone ads shown to anyone inside the convention center. Hold the pre-booked meetings, run the 60-second muscle test at the booth, and enter every visitor badge we scan into Salesforce the same day.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  3. Week 11 · February 9 to 15

    Follow up every conversation from the conference within 48 hours. First newsletter sponsorship goes live.

    Owner: sales, with the demand generation lead

  4. Weeks 12 and 13 · February 16 to 28

    Read the first pilot results against the measures each clinic wrote. Hold the Day 90 review of cost per qualified call, per pilot and per customer, by channel, role and test half.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales and leadership

Learn more about how the weekly call numbers are worked out

The weekly calls build from about 4 that already come in today to about 15 by Day 90: 4 to 7 a week in month one as search starts, 8 to 11 in month two as email and ads run together, and 12 to 15 in month three as spend moves to what works. Added up week by week, that is about 130 qualified calls by Day 90. At 55% to opportunities and 45% of those to pilots, about 30 pilots start or are scheduled.

The holidays fall in weeks 3 to 5, so month one builds and tests at low spend, and full launch waits for the first week of January.

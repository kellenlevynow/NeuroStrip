Weeks 1 to 4 · December 1 to 28

Set up

Day 30 check, December 30. The target is 6 to 8 qualified calls a week, and the ad platform in use must have found a named decision maker at 60% or more of the target groups. If it finds fewer, the ads would reach too few groups, so the ad budget is reallocated to search, email and the conference, and groups with 26 to 50 clinics are added. If calls are below 6 a week, the demand generation lead presents each channel's cost per qualified call to leadership, and the reserve is allocated only to channels under $930. Leadership decides.

  1. Week 1 · December 1 to 7

    We hold demos with four suppliers: Propensity (runs the account-based ads), HealthLink Dimensions and CampaignLake (sell provider lists) and Bombora (shows which groups are researching now). Each supplier counts the groups with 5 to 25 clinics it covers in our states. By December 7, we buy from the list supplier whose free sample names an owner or clinical director at more of the 50 groups Control Bionics already knows, with email and ad use confirmed in writing. We also listen to ten sales calls, interview customers from past trials, and agree on the five-point checklist with sales.

    Owner: the demand generation lead (the marketer who runs this plan), with sales

  2. Week 2 · December 8 to 14

    We buy the list and load it into Salesforce with each group's NPI, its list source, the topics Bombora shows it researching, and each contact's role. The lead stages, lead-source tracking and the routing rule that sends new leads to sales go live. Regulatory approves the list of permitted and prohibited claims about NeuroStrip by December 10. Google Search launches after that approval, by December 14.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the Salesforce partner and regulatory

  3. Week 3 · December 15 to 21

    We build the Propensity audience and randomly split the groups into the test half and the holdout half. By December 21, if Propensity matches fewer than 60% of groups to a named decision maker, the same list runs through PulsePoint or DeepIntent, and the test moves to whichever achieves the higher match rate. The team also builds the four landing pages, the 90-second video and the pilot guide, writes the message B version of each email sequence, and briefs the email agency.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the production studio

  4. Week 4 · December 22 to 28

    The account-based ads launch to the test half at low spend over the holidays. The first weekly live demo runs. The weekly report goes live, and a test lead passes through every form, email and calendar link to confirm that each step works.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

Weeks 5 to 8 · December 29 to January 25

Test

Day 60 check, January 29. The target is 10 to 12 qualified calls a week. The ads stop only if the test half is behind the holdout half on both interested replies and qualified calls per 100 groups. If the test half leads on both, the month-three increase proceeds. If it is level, or leads on only one, spend holds at the month-two level until Day 90. If calls are below 10 a week, the demand generation lead presents each channel's cost per qualified call to leadership, and the reserve is allocated only to channels under $930.

  1. Week 5 · December 29 to January 4

    The full launch takes place on Monday, January 4. Email starts to both halves, with groups researching now first. The persona tests begin, comparing message A with message B in the executive, operations and clinical clusters. A second test measures whether contacting a group's clinical leaders first, or its executives first, books more qualified calls.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the outbound email partner

  2. Week 6 · January 5 to 11

    Any ad version with no clicks from target groups after two weeks is paused, as is any keyword group that has spent $400 without a lead from a target group. The team reviews Google's report of what people actually typed, adds the productive searches as keywords and excludes the ones that waste spend. Meeting bookings for the conference begin.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  3. Week 7 · January 12 to 18

    We test which offer books more calls: a call to plan a pilot, or a live demo. The offer with more booked calls per 100 visitors wins once each version has about 300 visits, or at the end of week 10, whichever comes first. The first customer story is published if a customer has cleared it.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  4. Week 8 · January 19 to 25

    We read the persona tests once each message has reached about 150 people. If one message's rate of interested replies is at least 30% higher, that cluster adopts it for now. Every win this quarter is provisional and is tested again in month four. Clusters where neither message leads by 30% continue testing through six weeks, about 300 people per message. Ads and audiences are ranked by booked calls per 100 groups reached, and the bottom half is cut.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, reviewed with sales

Weeks 9 to 13 · January 26 to February 28

Scale

Day 90 review, Friday, February 26, with numbers through February 25. The targets are about 15 qualified calls a week, about 130 held in total and about 30 pilots started or scheduled. The ads retain their funding next quarter only if the test half books at least 20% more qualified calls per 100 groups than the holdout half at under $930 a call. A data or ad contract renews only if the channel it feeds costs under $930 a call. Next quarter's budget is allocated only to channels under $930, and more groups are added if the list is exhausted. Leadership decides.

  1. Week 9 · January 26 to February 1

    From January 30, after the Day 60 check, ad spend shifts to groups researching now if the ads retain their funding. Spend increases by up to 25% a week on any channel whose cost per qualified call is under the $930 limit. The remaining persona tests close by February 15, the end of week 11.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  2. Week 10 · February 2 to 8

    APTA CSM runs February 4 to 6 in Philadelphia, with mobile ads shown to anyone inside the convention center. The team holds the pre-booked meetings, runs the 60-second muscle test at the booth, and enters every scanned visitor badge into Salesforce the same day.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  3. Week 11 · February 9 to 15

    Every conversation from the conference receives a follow-up within 48 hours. The first newsletter sponsorship goes live and is booked again only if it delivers a qualified call for under $930.

    Owner: sales, with the demand generation lead

  4. Weeks 12 and 13 · February 16 to 28

    The team reads the first pilot results against the success measures each clinic wrote. The Day 90 review covers cost per qualified call, per pilot and per customer, by channel, role and test half.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales and leadership

Learn more about how the weekly call numbers are forecast

Weekly calls build from the roughly 4 that already arrive today to about 15 by Day 90. The ramp runs 4 to 8 a week in month one as search starts, 8 to 12 in month two as email and ads run together, and 12 to 15 in month three as spend shifts to what performs. Summed week by week, the ramp totals about 130 qualified calls by Day 90. At 55% to opportunities and 45% of those to pilots, about 30 pilots start or are scheduled.

Weeks 1 to 4 · December 1 to 28

Set up

Day 30 check, December 30. 6 to 8 qualified calls a week, and the ad platform in use has found a named decision maker at 60% or more of the target groups. If it finds fewer, the ads would reach too few groups, so the ad money moves to search, email and the conference, and we add groups with 26 to 50 clinics. If calls are below 6 a week, the demand generation lead shows leadership each channel's cost per qualified call, and the reserve goes only to channels under $930. Leadership decides.

  1. Week 1 · December 1 to 7

    Demos from four suppliers: Propensity (runs the account-based ads), HealthLink Dimensions and CampaignLake (sell provider lists) and Bombora (shows which groups are researching now). Each counts the groups with 5 to 25 clinics it covers in our states. By December 7, buy from the list supplier whose free sample names an owner or rehab director at more of 50 groups Control Bionics already knows, with email and ad use confirmed in writing. Listen to ten sales calls, interview customers from past trials, and agree the five-point checklist with sales.

    Owner: the demand generation lead (the marketer who runs this plan), with sales

  2. Week 2 · December 8 to 14

    Buy the list and load it into Salesforce with each group's NPI, which list it came from, what Bombora says it is researching, and each person's role. The steps a lead moves through, the record of where each lead came from and the rule that sends new leads to sales go live. Regulatory approves the list of what we may and may not say about NeuroStrip by December 10. Google Search launches once it is approved, by December 14.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the Salesforce partner and regulatory

  3. Week 3 · December 15 to 21

    Build the Propensity audience and split the groups at random into the test half and the holdout half. By December 21, if Propensity matches fewer than 60% of groups to a named decision maker, run the same list through PulsePoint or DeepIntent and move the test to whichever matches more. Build the four landing pages, the 90-second video and the pilot guide, write the message B version of each email sequence, and brief the email agency.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the production studio

  4. Week 4 · December 22 to 28

    Launch the account-based ads to the test half at low spend over the holidays. Run the first weekly live demo. The weekly report goes live, and a pretend lead goes through every form, email and calendar link to check each step works.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

Weeks 5 to 8 · December 29 to January 25

Test

Day 60 check, January 29. 10 to 12 qualified calls a week. The ads stop only if the test half is behind the holdout half on both interested replies and qualified calls per 100 groups. If it is ahead on both, the month-three increase goes ahead. If it is level, or ahead on only one, spend stays at the month-two level until Day 90. If calls are below 10 a week, the demand generation lead shows leadership each channel's cost per qualified call, and the reserve goes only to channels under $930.

  1. Week 5 · December 29 to January 4

    Full launch on Monday, January 4. Email starts to both halves, groups researching now first. The persona tests begin: message A against message B in the executive, operations and clinical clusters. We also test whether contacting a group's clinical leaders first, or its executives first, books more qualified calls.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with the outbound email partner

  2. Week 6 · January 5 to 11

    Pause any ad version with no clicks from target groups after two weeks, and any keyword group that has spent $400 without a lead from a target group. Read Google's report of what people actually typed, add the good searches as keywords and block the ones that waste money. Start booking meetings for the conference.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  3. Week 7 · January 12 to 18

    Test which offer books more calls: a call to plan a pilot, or a live demo. The offer with more booked calls per 100 visitors wins once each version has about 300 visits, or at the end of week 10, whichever comes first. Publish the first customer story, if a customer has cleared it.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  4. Week 8 · January 19 to 25

    Read the persona tests once each message has reached about 150 people. If one message's rate of interested replies is at least 30% higher, that cluster keeps it for now. Every win this quarter is provisional and is tested again in month four. Clusters where neither message is 30% ahead keep testing to six weeks, about 300 people a message. Rank ads and audiences by booked calls per 100 groups reached, and cut the bottom half.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, reviewed with sales

Weeks 9 to 13 · January 26 to February 28

Scale

Day 90 review, Friday, February 26, with numbers through February 25. About 15 qualified calls a week, about 130 held in total and about 30 pilots started or scheduled. The ads keep their money next quarter only if the test half books at least 20% more qualified calls per 100 groups than the holdout half at under $930 a call. A data or ad contract renews only if the channel it feeds costs under $930 a call. Next quarter's budget goes only to channels under $930, and more groups are added if the list is used up. Leadership decides.

  1. Week 9 · January 26 to February 1

    From January 30, after the Day 60 check: if the ads keep their money, move ad spend to groups researching now. Raise spend by up to 25% a week on any channel whose cost per qualified call is under the $930 limit. Remaining persona tests close by February 15, the end of week 11.

    Owner: the demand generation lead

  2. Week 10 · February 2 to 8

    APTA CSM, February 4 to 6 in Philadelphia, with phone ads shown to anyone inside the convention center. Hold the pre-booked meetings, run the 60-second muscle test at the booth, and enter every visitor badge we scan into Salesforce the same day.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales

  3. Week 11 · February 9 to 15

    Follow up every conversation from the conference within 48 hours. First newsletter sponsorship goes live, and is booked again only if it brings a qualified call for under $930.

    Owner: sales, with the demand generation lead

  4. Weeks 12 and 13 · February 16 to 28

    Read the first pilot results against the measures each clinic wrote. Hold the Day 90 review of cost per qualified call, per pilot and per customer, by channel, role and test half.

    Owner: the demand generation lead, with sales and leadership

Learn more about how the weekly call numbers are worked out

The weekly calls build from about 4 that already come in today to about 15 by Day 90: 4 to 8 a week in month one as search starts, 8 to 12 in month two as email and ads run together, and 12 to 15 in month three as spend moves to what works. Added up week by week, that is about 130 qualified calls by Day 90. At 55% to opportunities and 45% of those to pilots, about 30 pilots start or are scheduled.

The holidays fall in weeks 4 and 5, so month one builds and tests at low spend. Email and the persona tests start on Monday, January 4, the last day of week 5.
